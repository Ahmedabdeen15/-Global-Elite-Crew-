ISO 27020:2019 Preparation Service

Achieve ISO 27020:2019 Compliance with Our Expert Preparation Services

ISO 27020:2019 is an international standard titled "Dentistry — Brackets and tubes for use in orthodontics." This standard specifies requirements and test methods for evaluating the functional dimensions of orthodontic brackets and tubes, their chemical ion release, as well as packaging and labeling information. It applies to brackets and tubes used in fixed orthodontic appliances and ensures quality, safety, and performance standards are met in the dental industry.

Our Comprehensive Service Includes:

  • Gap Analysis: We conduct a thorough assessment of your current manufacturing processes, quality control systems, and product specifications to identify areas that require alignment with ISO 27020:2019 requirements for orthodontic brackets and tubes.
  • Technical Documentation Support: Assistance in preparing and organizing all necessary technical documentation, including specifications for functional dimensions, angles, chemical composition, ion release testing protocols, packaging requirements, and labeling information as specified in ISO 27020:2019.
  • Test Method Implementation: Expert guidance in implementing the required test methods for evaluating functional dimensions, chemical ion release, and compliance with hazardous element requirements as outlined in the standard.
  • Quality Control System Development: Development and implementation of quality control systems to ensure consistent compliance with dimensional requirements, chemical ion release limits, and packaging standards specified in ISO 27020:2019.
  • Employee Training: Comprehensive training programs for your technical staff, quality assurance team, and manufacturing personnel to ensure they understand and can effectively implement ISO 27020:2019 requirements in their daily operations.
  • Internal Audits: Pre-certification assessments and internal audits to ensure your manufacturing facility, testing laboratories, and quality systems are fully prepared for official ISO 27020:2019 compliance verification.
  • Compliance Coordination: We help you coordinate with certification bodies and testing laboratories to ensure proper verification of compliance with ISO 27020:2019 requirements, including functional dimension testing and chemical ion release analysis.
  • Ongoing Compliance Support: Continuous support to maintain ISO 27020:2019 compliance, including regular reviews of manufacturing processes, quality control procedures, and updates on any revisions to the standard.

ISO 27001 Preparation Service

Achieve ISO/IEC 27001 Certification with Our Expert Preparation Services

ISO/IEC 27001 is an international standard that specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS). It helps organizations manage the security of assets such as financial information, intellectual property, employee details, and information entrusted by third parties. The standard includes 93 security controls organized into four categories: organizational controls, people controls, physical controls, and technological controls, ensuring comprehensive protection against evolving cyber threats.

Our Comprehensive Service Includes:

  • ISMS Gap Analysis: We conduct a comprehensive assessment of your current information security practices, policies, and procedures to identify gaps and areas requiring alignment with ISO/IEC 27001 requirements, including risk assessment processes and security control implementation.
  • ISMS Documentation Development: Assistance in developing and organizing all necessary ISMS documentation, including information security policies, risk assessment reports, Statement of Applicability (SOA), risk treatment plans, procedures for security controls, and incident management protocols.
  • Risk Assessment and Treatment: Expert guidance in conducting systematic information security risk assessments, identifying threats and vulnerabilities, evaluating impacts, and developing risk treatment plans aligned with Annex A controls and your organization's risk appetite.
  • Security Controls Implementation: Implementation support for the 93 security controls across organizational (A.5), people (A.6), physical (A.7), and technological (A.8) categories, including access control, cryptography, network security, incident management, and business continuity planning.
  • Employee Training and Awareness: Comprehensive training programs on information security awareness, ISMS requirements, security policies and procedures, incident response, and role-specific security responsibilities to ensure your workforce understands and adheres to ISO/IEC 27001 standards.
  • Internal ISMS Audits: Pre-certification internal audits to assess ISMS effectiveness, verify control implementation, identify non-conformities, and ensure your organization is fully prepared for the official ISO/IEC 27001 certification audit.
  • Management Review Support: Assistance in conducting management reviews of the ISMS, including performance evaluation, monitoring and measurement of security controls, and ensuring top management commitment to continual improvement.
  • Certification Audit Coordination: We help you select an accredited certification body and coordinate the external ISO/IEC 27001 certification audit process, including Stage 1 (documentation review) and Stage 2 (on-site audit), ensuring a smooth path to certification.
  • Ongoing ISMS Maintenance: Continuous support to maintain ISO/IEC 27001 certification, including regular security reviews, control effectiveness monitoring, incident response support, and assistance with surveillance audits and recertification.

ISO/IEC 17020 Preparation Service

Achieve ISO/IEC 17020 Accreditation with Our Expert Preparation Services

ISO/IEC 17020 is an international standard that specifies requirements for the competence of bodies performing inspection activities and for the impartiality and consistency of their inspection operations. It applies to various types of inspection bodies (Type A, B, or C) and covers all stages of inspection, from initial assessment through reporting. The standard ensures that inspection bodies demonstrate independence, impartiality, technical competence, and have adequate resources and management systems to deliver reliable inspection services.

Our Comprehensive Service Includes:

  • Gap Analysis: We conduct a thorough assessment of your current inspection operations, organizational structure, technical capabilities, and management systems to identify areas requiring alignment with ISO/IEC 17020 requirements, including independence classification and impartiality management.
  • Organizational Structure Development: Assistance in establishing and documenting the appropriate organizational structure, defining roles and responsibilities, ensuring adequate resources, and implementing management systems required by ISO/IEC 17020 for your inspection body type (A, B, or C).
  • Impartiality and Independence Management: Expert guidance in establishing and maintaining impartiality policies, managing conflicts of interest, ensuring independence from commercial or financial pressures, and documenting the independence classification of your inspection body.
  • Inspection Procedures and Methods: Development and documentation of inspection procedures, methods, and techniques specific to your inspection scope, including criteria for inspection, sampling methods, measurement techniques, and acceptance/rejection criteria.
  • Technical Competence Development: Support in ensuring your inspection personnel have the necessary qualifications, training, experience, and technical competence to perform inspections effectively, including maintaining records of personnel competence and training programs.
  • Equipment and Facilities Management: Guidance in establishing procedures for equipment calibration, maintenance, and verification, ensuring inspection equipment is suitable, properly maintained, and traceable to national or international standards.
  • Inspection Reporting System: Development of standardized inspection report formats and procedures that clearly communicate inspection findings, results, and conclusions in accordance with ISO/IEC 17020 requirements.
  • Management System Documentation: Assistance in preparing and organizing all necessary management system documentation, including quality manual, procedures, work instructions, records management, and document control systems.
  • Internal Audits and Management Reviews: Pre-accreditation internal audits and management review support to assess compliance with ISO/IEC 17020, identify non-conformities, and ensure your inspection body is fully prepared for the official accreditation assessment.
  • Accreditation Coordination: We help you select an appropriate accreditation body and coordinate the ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation process, including application preparation, document review, on-site assessment, and follow-up activities to achieve accreditation.
  • Ongoing Compliance Support: Continuous support to maintain ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation, including assistance with surveillance assessments, scope extensions, handling of complaints and appeals, and ensuring continual improvement of your inspection operations.
